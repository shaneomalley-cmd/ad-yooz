02How it works

Routing an invoice through approval

Four things happen automatically, in order, every time an invoice reaches the queue.

01

Invoice enters the queue

A captured invoice routes into approval automatically — no one has to assign it manually.

02

Routes in parallel or by line

Multiple approvers act at the same time, or individual invoice lines route to whoever owns that cost centre.

03

Thresholds and delegation apply

Approval limits enforce on their own. An out-of-office approver's queue reassigns without a manual handoff.

04

Every action is recorded

A timestamped audit trail forms underneath the queue — nothing to reconstruct after the fact.

04Proof, not a promise

The configuration underneath

Depth is the proof. Here's what's actually configurable.

call_split

Parallel & line-level routing

Route a single invoice to multiple approvers at once, or split it by line so each cost-centre owner only approves their portion.

switch_account

Delegation

An approver going out of office reassigns their queue to a named backup for a set date range. The audit trail still shows who actually approved.

rule

Thresholds

Approval limits by role, department, or dollar amount enforce automatically — an invoice above a manager's limit routes up on its own.

history

Audit trail

Every routing decision, delegation, and approval is timestamped and retained — the record an auditor asks for already exists.
