The Z device

Placeholder geometry only — flat Rich Blue→Pink gradient fill, correct anchoring and aspect ratio. Real photography is graded (desaturate → curves → pink/teal gradient map → noise) and cut to this same silhouette in production.

Grey 10 bounding box

The brand's structural device on a patterned ground. Vary size/inset by importance — don't chop every section into identically-sized cards.

Small — inline callouts, form cards

Medium — standard content section

Large — hero content box, the page's primary claim
