02Compare your options

Four ways to close the AP gap

Whatever accounts payable system you land on, it's one of these four. Laid out here at equal weight, including the one Yooz doesn't sell.

account_balance

Your ERP's native AP module

The AP entry-and-payment screen that came bundled with your accounting system or ERP.

Good fit: one entity, one ERP, low invoice volume, simple approvals.

Watch for: manual entry, approvals that can't reach outside finance, no fraud or duplicate-payment screening.

handshake

Outsourcing AP

A bookkeeping firm or outsourced AP provider processes invoices and payments for you.

Good fit: you want AP off your team's plate and can accept less day-to-day visibility.

Watch for: per-invoice fees that scale with volume, and less control over timing and vendor relationships.

person_add

Hiring more AP staff

Add headcount to keep pace with a growing stack of invoices, using the process you already have.

Good fit: the volume spike is temporary, or the process itself isn't the bottleneck.

Watch for: cost scales with volume, and manual-entry errors and late payments don't go away — they just get more hands.

bolt

A dedicated AP automation layer

Software that sits on top of your existing ERP to capture, route, and reconcile invoices automatically.

Good fit: volume, entity count, or approval complexity has outgrown a native module or extra hands.

Watch for: it's a new system to connect and learn — less manual work in exchange for setup time.

This page argues for the fourth option in the section below — but if the first one already covers what you need, the honest answer is to stay on it.

03The honest case

Where a dedicated layer starts to pay for itself

A native module and an extra hire can both work — for a while. Yooz doesn't replace your ERP; it layers on top of it, so the accounting system you already run stays the system of record while the AP-specific work gets automated. It connects to more than 250 financial systems, so a later ERP change doesn't mean rebuilding your AP process from scratch.

A dedicated layer is worth the setup when any of these is true:

  • You run more than one entity, subsidiary, or ERP and need one AP process across all of them.
  • Approvals need to route to managers or department heads outside the finance team.
  • Your invoice volume has outgrown what manual entry or a native module can keep up with.
  • You need a searchable audit trail and duplicate-payment or fraud screening your ERP wasn't built to provide.
