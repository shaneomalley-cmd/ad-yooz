How Long Does Implementation Take?expand_more

Yooz uses different implementation paths depending on project scope. Eligible standard deployments can follow the fixed-scope ReadyToYooz Starter framework. More complex projects use an Extended framework covering discovery, design, integration, configuration, validation, training, user acceptance testing, go-live coordination, and hypercare. Timing depends on ERP access, data complexity, workflow requirements, optional capabilities, testing, and stakeholder availability. Your project plan is confirmed after those requirements are reviewed.

Is Yooz Secure and Compliant?expand_more

Yooz production-platform controls are audited under SOC 1 Type 2. The opinion letter is available through Yooz's security-document process, and the full report may be provided subject to confidentiality requirements. Where Yooz processes customer data that includes personal data, Yooz complies with applicable personal-data protection obligations, including the GDPR.
