New to AP automation

What AP automation actually does

AP automation replaces manual invoice handling — routing PDFs and paper by email or hand, chasing approvers, keying data into your ERP — with a connected six-step flow:

  • 1Purchase
  • 2Capture
  • 3Review
  • 4Approve
  • 5Pay
  • 6Export

Capture alone spans email, drag & drop, mobile, scan, sFTP, e-invoicing, fetcher and API — whatever format an invoice actually arrives in. Yooz exports to more than 250 financial systems, so "Export" connects to the ERP you already run, not a new one.
