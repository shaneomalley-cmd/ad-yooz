What Is Touchless Invoice Processing?

Touchless invoice processing is when an invoice moves from arrival to payment without anyone keying its data in by hand. The software reads the invoice, checks it against what was ordered and received, and routes it for payment on its own — a person only gets involved if something doesn't match. It's not a separate product; it's the outcome of AP automation software working the way it's supposed to on a "clean" invoice.

How an invoice goes touchless

"Touchless" describes what happens to an invoice across the same six stages every AP automation platform runs — it isn't a different process, just this one running with no manual intervention:

  1. 1Purchase
  2. →
  3. 2Capture
  4. →
  5. 3Review
  6. →
  7. 4Approve
  8. →
  9. 5Pay
  10. →
  11. 6Export
Where touchless is decided — Capture & Review Runs the same either way

Capture is where the invoice's data gets read — from whichever channel it arrived on: email, drag & drop, mobile, scan, sFTP, e-invoicing, fetcher or API. Review is where that data is checked against the purchase order and the receipt (the 3-way match). If both stages complete clean — the data reads correctly and the match holds — the invoice never needs a human hand on it until an approver signs off at the Approve stage, which can itself be rules-based for invoices under a set threshold.

What takes an invoice out of touchless

No AP platform gets every invoice through Capture and Review untouched — and a platform that claims otherwise is a red flag, not a feature. An invoice drops out of the touchless path when:

  • The invoice has no matching purchase order, or the PO was raised after the fact.
  • The captured data doesn't reconcile with the PO or receipt — a price, quantity, or vendor mismatch.
  • The vendor is new, or their invoice format hasn't been seen by the system before.
  • An approval rule requires a named person's sign-off regardless of match status.

The realistic goal isn't "100% touchless" — it's shrinking the list above so exceptions become the minority of invoices instead of the majority, and the exceptions that remain reach a human with the context already assembled instead of a blank invoice and a filing cabinet.
